AI Stack Portal Intern / Junior Associate (1–2 Roles)
Location: Bengaluru (Work from Office – Lifesight Innovation Hub)
Duration: Internship (with potential for full-time conversion)
Role Context
As organizations increasingly integrate artificial intelligence into core workflows, there is a growing need to operationalize AI tools in a structured and scalable manner. This role is positioned at the intersection of AI adoption, internal tooling, and operational enablement, with a focus on managing and evolving the organization’s AI Stack Portal.
The selected candidates will contribute to ensuring that AI tools are not only deployed but also actively utilized, maintained, and continuously improved across teams.
Role Objective
The primary objective of this role is to manage, maintain, and enhance the AI Stack Portal, enabling seamless access, usability, and adoption of AI tools across the organization.
Key Responsibilities
Manage the AI Stack Portal, including updates, access control, and tool integration
Track and monitor AI tool usage, adoption metrics, and engagement trends
Support onboarding of teams onto AI tools and workflows
Coordinate with internal stakeholders (Product, Engineering, GTM teams) to ensure relevance of tools
Identify gaps in current AI stack and recommend improvements
Maintain documentation, guides, and usage playbooks for internal users
Assist in testing and deploying new AI tools, features, or workflows
Ensure data hygiene, compliance, and structured reporting within the portal

Engineering (CS/IT/AI/Data Science)

Comfort working in a fast-paced, experimentation-driven environment
